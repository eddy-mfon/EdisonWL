## Frequently asked questions

### Is Edison only for students?

No. Edison is built for people who want a calmer place for useful work. Students can keep learning on track, while growing teams can keep notes and next steps close.

### What can I put in Edison?

Start with the small things that usually get scattered: notes, ideas, deadlines, customer calls, tasks, and the next move.

### Do I need to move everything at once?

No. Start with one class, one project, or one team thread. Edison is meant to make the next step feel lighter, not create another big setup job.

### When will Edison be ready?

We are building with early users now. Join the waitlist and we will share useful updates as Edison takes shape.
